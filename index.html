About

This fishy app was made by Ivan to study vocabulary. Because all flashcard apps I tried were simply shit. Flashker Ultimate by Ivan is a local vocabulary app for focused learning and review. Organize your own words, choose a study method, and track your progress through a simple interface. Version 1.0.3. Codename: Flounder, a BEAST project.

How to use

1. Build your vocabulary. Import or add words in Vocabulary Decks, then assign tags to organize your study lists.
2. Start studying. Open Study Lists and choose a list to learn or review.
3. Choose your mode. Select the method that suits your practice. The four study modes are described in Features.
4. Use the gestures. Swipe left for Wrong, swipe right for Correct, tap to flip, and long-press to edit.

Recall the answer before revealing it. Use Undo to reverse your last answer and Quit to finish. Tap the Previous cards area to hide or restore session details and controls.

Your progress follows each word across all its study lists. Export a JSON backup from Settings to keep a separate copy of your vocabulary and learning history.

Food for thought: One problem with spaced repetition methods is that they usually schedule words based on time (minutes/days). Therefore, the scheduler will often make you go through your whole deck once, and then tell you that all words are due tomorrow or in a few days. I do not find this useful for acquiring brand new vocabulary from scratch. The idea of the IVAN study mode algorithm is to rely on rote memory: high-frequency high-speed repetition to force automatic recall. Try to go through as many cards as quickly as you can! For optimal results, I usually keep study lists of around 48 words (8 chunks of 6 word cards each). Same as other methods, the words must be reviewed again several times, with pause breaks, but within a short time frame! In IVAN study mode, a Wrong first answer reveals the card; forcing the user to acknowledge this has demonstrated benefits for memory retention. Scientific studies do not necessarily support or reject many of the choices made for default parameters, such as chunk size and short penalty intervals. But I know that it works for me.

Features

Your vocabulary. Your pace.
• Elegant, uncluttered interface with touch and keyboard controls.
• Import vocabulary from CSV, pasted text, or manual entry; export decks as CSV.
• Tags that organize study lists without duplicating cards.
• Four study modes, including adaptive, Anki-inspired spaced repetition and automatic next-review scheduling.
• Adjustable themes, fonts, flip animations, pronunciation, and study settings.
• Up to ten local profiles with independent progress and statistics.
• Offline study after an initial online visit, plus complete JSON backup export and restore.

Choose your study mode
BASIC: Continuous practice; missed cards return sooner, while correct cards move to the end. Choose it for a flexible practice session without waiting for due dates.
IVAN HCC-SRS (Queue Mastery): Structured practice in small groups, alternating recall directions and advancing through Leitner boxes; FSRS calculates next-review dates while practice continues independently of them. In Flashker, HCC stands for Hierarchical Chunk Consolidation: small card groups are combined into progressively larger review groups.
SRS: Box-based spaced repetition with adjustable review intervals and penalties for missed answers. Correct answers advance a card to the next box; a first failure moves it back and starts immediate relearning.
FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler): Adaptive spaced repetition that uses your review history to calculate when to review again, with Again, Hard, Good, and Easy ratings. Adjust desired retention to balance review frequency; higher targets mean more frequent reviews.

Local profiles share vocabulary but keep their own learning records and settings. No account or cloud sync is required.

Options for study mode IVAN HCC-SRS Saved per profile; a session uses a frozen copy. Small queues shorten the spacing. Continue grants another answer allowance. IVAN practices the selected list; FSRS schedules its next due dates.
Options for study mode SRS
Default: 100%. Range: 50–200%. Scales the existing box intervals: 50% changes 14 days to 7 days; 200% changes it to 28 days. Successful relearning still has a one-day minimum. Default: 2 boxes. Range: 1–3. A larger loss moves the card farther back on its first failure. Immediate relearning stays available; repeated failures during relearning do not subtract more boxes. Saved per profile with Save Settings. Changes apply to future SRS answers; existing deadlines and saved boxes are not rewritten.
Options for study mode FSRS
Default: 90%. Range: 80–97%. The scheduler aims for this recall rate: higher targets mean more frequent reviews; lower targets mean fewer reviews. This is a target, not a guarantee. Saved per profile with Save Settings. Applies to future FSRS and IVAN next-review deadlines because they share FSRS memory. Existing deadlines are not rewritten. IVAN's untimed practice queue is unchanged.
